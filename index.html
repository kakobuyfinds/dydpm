How to use the HipoBuy spreadsheet

Before you order

  1. Open the HipobuyFind listing and compare its title, photos, selected variant and size chart with the underlying seller listing.
  2. If you plan to use HipoBuy, confirm it accepts the seller link and that your chosen variant can ship to your destination.
  3. If warehouse quality-control (QC) photos are available, check measurements, color and visible defects before approving shipment.
  4. Compare quotes for your destination using the packed parcel dimensions and weight. Check the carrier’s volumetric weight rules.

Estimate your order total

Convert your quotes to one currency before adding them. Use your own prices and enter 0 for costs that do not apply.

HipoBuy spreadsheet FAQ

Where do the catalog entries come from?

The names and item IDs come from a snapshot of the HipobuyFind catalog. Each listing link opens the matching item on HipobuyFind.

Where do the two top buttons go?

Both buttons open the HipobuyFind homepage, where you can browse the full catalog. If you want to order using HipoBuy, check whether your chosen seller link is supported there.

Do the listed prices include international shipping?

The cards show reference item prices. Estimate the full cost with a shipping quote for your destination, plus any applicable domestic shipping, fees and taxes.

What should I check in QC photos?

Check the exact variant, color, measurements and visible condition. For shoes, request an insole measurement if sizing is uncertain. For clothing, compare flat measurements against a garment you already own.
